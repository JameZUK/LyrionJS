PLUGIN_LYRIONJS
	EN	LyrionJS

PLUGIN_LYRIONJS_DESC
	EN	Play music in any web browser: the browser becomes a Slimproto player in LMS, with a full controller. Open it at /plugins/LyrionJS/ on this server.

PLUGIN_LYRIONJS_LINK
	EN	LyrionJS web player

PLUGIN_LYRIONJS_MENU_INFO
	EN	Tap the address to open the player, or open it in a browser on any device on your network.

PLUGIN_LYRIONJS_OPEN
	EN	Web player

PLUGIN_LYRIONJS_OPEN_DESC
	EN	Open the player in a browser on any device on your network: that browser becomes a player in LMS. Bookmark the address, or add it to the home screen on a phone or tablet.

PLUGIN_LYRIONJS_OPEN_BUTTON
	EN	Open LyrionJS

PLUGIN_LYRIONJS_URL
	EN	Player address

PLUGIN_LYRIONJS_URL_DESC
	EN	The address LMS's menus and links open the player at. Leave empty to use this server's name from DNS (or its IP address if it has none). Set it if browsers reach LMS under another name, such as a reverse proxy: the player is one LMS player per browser and address, so keep to one.

PLUGIN_LYRIONJS_URL_BAD
	EN	The player address must be a plain http:// or https:// address, such as http://music.example.lan:9000. It was not changed.

PLUGIN_LYRIONJS_ENABLED
	EN	Browser players

PLUGIN_LYRIONJS_ENABLED_DESC
	EN	Allow browsers to connect as players. When off, the page still works as a controller for your other players.

PLUGIN_LYRIONJS_PROXY
	EN	Radio and podcast streams

PLUGIN_LYRIONJS_PROXY_DESC
	EN	Fetch internet streams through LMS for LyrionJS players (recommended: a browser can't fetch most stations directly). Sets LMS's own "proxied" streaming option on LyrionJS players only, when they connect.

PLUGIN_LYRIONJS_OUTPUT
	EN	Audio output

PLUGIN_LYRIONJS_OUTPUT_DESC
	EN	How browsers play, unless a browser has been set otherwise in its own settings. Automatic uses the media element on phones and tablets (keeps playing with the screen locked) and Web Audio elsewhere.

PLUGIN_LYRIONJS_OUTPUT_AUTO
	EN	Automatic

PLUGIN_LYRIONJS_OUTPUT_WEBAUDIO
	EN	Web Audio (lowest latency)

PLUGIN_LYRIONJS_OUTPUT_MEDIA
	EN	Media element (plays with the screen locked)

PLUGIN_LYRIONJS_DIAG
	EN	Diagnostics

PLUGIN_LYRIONJS_DIAG_DESC
	EN	Let browser players write playback diagnostics to the LMS log (useful when something doesn't play on a phone or tablet).

PLUGIN_LYRIONJS_PLAYERS
	EN	LyrionJS players

PLUGIN_LYRIONJS_NO_PLAYERS
	EN	No browser has connected yet.

PLUGIN_LYRIONJS_CONNECTED
	EN	connected

PLUGIN_LYRIONJS_DISCONNECTED
	EN	not connected

PLUGIN_LYRIONJS_SESSIONS
	EN	Open player connections
